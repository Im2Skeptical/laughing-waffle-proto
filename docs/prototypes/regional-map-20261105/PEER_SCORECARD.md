# Peer scorecard

Scores are a qualitative design audit, not measured task performance. Images and
official manual are the evidence; no claims about unseen states. Civ VI's manual
is an enduring design reference, not a claim about the latest game's current UI.

| Dimension | Civilization VI | Old World |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Task clarity | 4 | 4 |
| Structure & hierarchy | 4 | 4 |
| Layout efficiency | 4 | 4 |
| Signal vs noise | 3 | 3 |
| Consistency | 4 | 4 |
| Feedback & status | 4 | 4 |
| Expert efficiency | 4 | 4 |
| Trust of data | 4 | 4 |
| World readability | 4 | 5 |
| Place comparison | 4 | 4 |
| Total | 39/50 | 40/50 |

Below-4 evidence: Civ VI's many yield/unit symbols compete with its city banners
in the official Interface image; Old World's top resource strip and auxiliary
portraits are dense even though the map image remains dominant. No score of 5
is assigned to invisible interaction behavior. Old World's map readability 5
refers specifically to the published Roma map view, not every game situation.

Benchmark: 40/50. Peer medians: 4 in each dimension except signal/noise 3 and
world readability 4.5. Baseline prototype 25/50.
