# Peer brief — 2026-11-05

Surface: Civilization Survivor regional overview, settlement focus, and selected
economy on 844×390 phone landscape / 1440×900 desktop. Key jobs: orient in the
world, find our holdings and valuable targets, understand political security,
inspect a settlement's engine, return to the map.

Peers: Civilization VI (2K/Firaxis official manual, Interface / city interaction)
and Old World (Mohawk Games official media screenshot / official manual).
Civ VII's current marketing images were inspected but have no HUD; they cannot
support UI scores and are not counted as a scored peer.

- Civ VI uses a compact city banner as a place identifier; selecting the place
  provides management detail. Resource/yield glyphs can still overwhelm a map:
  copy the information hierarchy, not the total icon density.
- Old World exposes identity/progress near Roma, relegates global bookkeeping
  to screen edges, and keeps terrain, city architecture and unit silhouettes as
  the visual field. Its dense global resource strip is not our target.
- Design inference: map identity first, local readable numbers second, detailed
  ledgers only on deliberate focus. A repeated multi-row stats panel on every
  settlement flattens that hierarchy even when every number is accurate.
