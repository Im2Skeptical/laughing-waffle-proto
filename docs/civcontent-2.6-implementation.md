## Authoritative Charge resolver

- Each Practice owns an integer Charge meter. Stock plans never read Charge. Structures contain passive data and never own Charge.
- Positive Stock generation emits one event per actual yield, with source Practice, tags, Traits, staffing and region. Atomic Practice consumption emits one event per recipe with the actual consumed Traits/providers. OR triggers match one event once. Capacity-clipped zero yields emit no Stock-generation event.
- Full meters cap further gains. Every legal Discharge performs its effects without paying or requiring Stock, resets to zero and can produce new events. Workers multiply incoming Charge by `1 + effectiveWorkers * workerBonus` (including eligible passive Charge gains, rounded down); they do not multiply Discharge output. A completely full Stock host blocks output Discharge; available partial output room is legal. Unmet specialist staffing, population or external conditions keep full Charge intact. Stock-recipe-gated Structure bonuses no longer match Charge Practices; trait-based capacity and other eligible bonuses still apply.
- Root chains drain in initiating-event order, with FIFO events inside each chain. All matching Charge gains for an event happen before ready Practices resolve in authored region/tableau order against current state. A blocked earlier Practice can retry when a later event frees output capacity or satisfies its non-Stock condition. Scheduled activations, phase events, deaths, external conflicts/trade and Vassal actions use this same queue. Newly full cards are never resolved by a separate forecast or test resolver.
- Each Practice may Discharge once in a root chain. Refilled Charge stays on the Practice for a subsequent local root event. The global safety cap stops the remaining root events and records a **cascadeSafetyCap** diagnostic. There is no card-specific anti-loop wording.
- Bounded JSON traces retain root ID, parent event ID, region, source/target Practice, gains, payments, Discharges, blocked reasons and safeguard deferrals. No new RNG calls, skipped ticks, UI rules or forecast implementation were introduced.
- Upgrades/reordering preserve an existing Practice's Charge and Stock. A confirmed shop change retries full blocked cards through a real settlement-change event.

Banked effects are general DSL operations: next-candidate Development, next-shop quality, next-conflict Support/coordination, capped Crisis preview detail and seasonal Food bonuses. Candidate Development is consumed by real candidate generation; quality uplift is consumed by the real next Scholar content shop; Support banks are spent by real Campaign/Defense. Rescue Parties exposes a real Crisis evacuation that preserves demographic/specialist composition. Walls of the Last Refuge can evacuate a share of population into available connected Housing before territorial loss. Plague House reduces premature-death Chaos pressure without changing the actual death counts or RNG rolls.

## Remaining deviations and reasons

No rows or Charge assignments are omitted. The following behavior remains provisional; the card-by-card tables above record the actual executable replacement and all affected secondary hooks.

1. **Provider position:** the workbook sometimes says “to the left.” This pass preserves the user's explicitly required existing board-wide Consume/Require semantics, choosing providers in left-to-right order, including the consumer and providers to its right. Source prose remains intact in the source record; runtime card rules say “on the board.” Library's position bypass is consequently already inherent in the shared provider rule.
2. **Development, candidate weighting and Legacy hooks:** banked Development becomes capped starting Ingenuity/Prowess on the next eligible generated candidate. Institutional hooks use existing gated starting-stat bonuses and capped history bonuses. Choice conversion, special Development biases, class sampling weights, Chairs, Tomes/Chronicles, additional retirement choices, special Legacy outcomes and special opening Life Map node mixes are deferred because they require expanding the existing choice/Legacy/content-generation interfaces. This is a shared numerical implementation, not bespoke behavior for each card.
3. **Action type/range specialization:** Siege Training uses supplied +25% Support and a small bank; Forced Marches banks local Support; Warband Marching/War Council/Great Host and networking institutions use capped connected-friendly contributions (25% for coordination, 50% for Great Host, at most 10 per source). Separate Raid/Conquest/Monster weighting, longer travel/route reach, intermediate Mobility routing, special collateral/infrastructure preservation and reduced travel times remain deferred because the current conflict/travel interfaces do not distinguish those supply channels. Formation's primary +2 next-conflict bank and real support events are implemented.
4. **Preview/Discovery/Commission variants:** Watchkeeping, Plague Ledger, Doomsaying, Rescue preparation and Map Room use capped live-Crisis detail/incident breadth. Future imminent-failure sourcing, better automatic target choice, extra Discovery/Development/Commission options and per-institution reward/objective variants are deferred to the upcoming inspection/action UX. Experimentation's Research and next-shop quality uplift are implemented; esoteric Discovery/Legacy menus are not expanded here.
5. **Secondary population/culture and institutional scaling:** Feasting trains Warriors and banks candidate development, without extra Happiness/Faith or class weighting. Saga Keeping generates Records and banks development. Abstract institutional “stronger/capped” effects use the explicit provisional coefficients in the tables. Historical sources (including age, Chaos, losses, retired class Vassals, their Commissions and stocked Records) provide capped candidate stats rather than amplifying arbitrary institutional effects. Cross-settlement printing checks, chosen-Trait Storehouse configuration, arbitrary standardized multi-chain Commission checks, Astronomy's all-season Research reward, and complete species/retirement narrative clauses remain deferred; these need broader generalized interfaces than this Charge/content pass. Existing flat purchase prices and worker multiplication remain provisional. The follow-up art pass supplies dedicated paintings for all 187 runtime rows, preserving existing card paintings.
6. **Emergency rescue:** Rescue Parties moves up to five people with specialist identities intact to a connected friendly settlement with free Housing. It does not transfer hosted Stock or add a personal dangerous-martial rescue branch. Walls rescue a capped population share rather than introducing a separate refugee-survival system.
7. **Effective uniqueness:** existing Structure placement permits duplicates. History capstones use capped/shared numerical bonuses rather than a new unique-institution inventory rule. Lyceum's completed founding behavior is preserved. Unique retirement/Legacy rewards are covered by deviation 2.
