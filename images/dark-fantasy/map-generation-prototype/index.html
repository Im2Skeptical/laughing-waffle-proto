All map graphics and controls are in the Pixi window. Fullscreen keeps the game’s fixed landscape format. Atlas uses new map-scale landmarks with overview, regional and close detail. Zoom smoothly to see them change; Detail can force each level. Compare Smooth zoom and Nearest pixels. Sites cycles new map art, old game assets, sketch markers and off. Water, fog, clouds and shadows follow the timegraph. Drag to preview history or forecast, play forward or reverse, change speed, and use Now to return. Space pauses, arrow keys step one second (Shift: ten), and N returns to now. The graph uses a sample timeline; no gameplay is attached. Use the Fog, Clouds, Wisps and Shadows buttons to compare layers independently. Wisps adds the original cloud field at low opacity; Shadows includes its moving shade over playable land, even with Wisps off. Tap any region to inspect it.
