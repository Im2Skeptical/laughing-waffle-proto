# Chaos-god enemy art pass

Cam accepted Merchant green/copper-orange and requested another pass on the enemy
card set, especially composition and value hierarchy. This study covers the 80
enemy institutions plus eight capital rites/supports: 22 per enemy class, 52
Practices and 36 Structures. The original 20 player and 30 neutral paintings,
all descriptive rules and all simulation definitions remain unchanged.

Open `#/dev/prototypes?study=full-map-content&look=chaos&samples=1`. The existing
Zoo renderer, real frame/fittings, full reading, filters, session review notes and
export are reused inside Prototypes. `samples=1` shows all88 enemy cards; clearing
it adds the unchanged50 player/neutral candidates (138 total). Pool filters show
22 per class. Earlier artwork remains available in the appearance selector.

## Shared direction

| Enemy class | Settled primary/accent | Proposed patron grammar |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warlord | Vermilion/brick red; oxidized teal | Unbending Crown: three rigid upright spear-prongs bound by one closed band; obedience makes an armoured body |
| Merchant | Olive/forest green; copper-orange | Last Creditor: asymmetric hooked grasp around one solid disc; property becomes captivity and appetite |
| Priest | Reference warm straw yellow; rose pink | Hollow Sun: empty ring with three falling rays, never an eye; belonging becomes surrender |
| Philosopher | Cobalt/lapis blue; honey gold | Unfinished Index: broad open book and one hooked binding spine; classification replaces the person |

Names and gods remain proposals. Cult marks are not faction allegiance. On
frontiers a worn fitting hints at the patron; provincial mechanisms institutionalise
the shape; capitals become inhabited god-bodies. The institution's useful function
must remain recognisable. The presence is inherited/local, not an active invading
enemy or replacement for the larger monster threat. No card without an existing
population cost gains a sacrifice mechanic through this art pass.

## Composition and value acceptance

- Practice: opaque5:7; complete action, people, hands and material in the upper
  central45%. Lower half contains visible, coherent areas of rest: broad cloth,
  water, floor, terrain or architectural forms at restrained contrast. Never a
  blank black field or artificial fade. Vary viewpoint, grouping, gesture and
  setting; do not repeat the raised walkway and giant arch across a set.
- Structure: opaque3:4 /3:2 /9:4 for one/two/three cells; named building dominates
  central75%, people tiny anonymous dots. Clear silhouette before ornament.
- One focal area with strongest light/contrast; broad connected value masses.
  Resting areas may be light or middle-valued. No fixed dark/light percentage.
  Restrained texture and edge highlights. Sky, debris, banners and peripheral
  masonry must not compete with the action or entrance.
- N.C.Wyeth/Dean Cornwell/Howard Pyle narrative composition with hard pixel clusters and
  selective dithering. All living/dead/sculpted faces indiscernible at full size.
  No readable writing, UI, border or giant pasted crest. Diegetic cult shapes are
  the explicit user-requested exception to the ordinary no-symbol illustration rule.

Cam accepted the36 Structures but rejected the first52 Practice compositions as
too similar and too blank/dark below. The revised Practice pass uses otherworldly
corpse-built machinery and foundations: ribs, spines and anonymous mummified
bodies physically construct the institution. It retains useful actions and class
patron grammar rather than placing incidental skeletons beside an ordinary scene.
Skeleton heads remain wrapped, capped, buried or turned away; no readable faces.
The shared current guide also records Merchant green/copper, Priest yellow/pink,
and inherited remnant-neutral decay for future art. No neutral repaint is included.

This directly serves pillar3 (visible institutional transformation). Recognisable
functions support pillar1's interacting engines and pillar2's choices between
inherited components. Cosmic ornament that hides function strains both. For
pillar4, legible costs and inspection support understanding consequences, but
this catalogue does not project or simulate them. Earlier civilisation remains
support pillar5's theme of inheritance; no legacy mechanic is implemented. State,
RNG, schemas, replay and runtime registries are untouched.

## Provenance and isolation

Every painting is a dedicated built-in `image_gen.imagegen` call. `practice-rest-prompts.json`
records the revised52 Practice briefs; `practice-rest-corrections.json` records
targeted corrections. `prompts.json` preserves the preceding88-card pass, with
Practice entries marked as superseded. `initial-prompts.json`
preserves the eight rejected reference-led briefs; `generation-results.jsonl`
records sources, outputs and SHA-256 hashes, including superseded attempts. The
latest record for an ID describes its current source. Rejected sources remain
in ignored `artifacts/full-map-chaos/rejected/` and all earlier study assets stay
intact. Corrections, if needed, receive their own exact prompt records.
`current-assets.json` indexes each current source hash and its exact prompt record,
including accepted fresh anchors and corrective edits.

The four on-demand class atlases use unique `chaos_<card-id>` illustration IDs.
Only this appearance mode loads them; ordinary game registries and previous
projections keep their earlier IDs. No PR, merge, publication or gameplay promotion
is part of this study. Population-consuming institutions retain the existing
review proposals and Cam's agreed ability to keep their power and cost after capture.

Verification must cover full-size source acceptance, card-size/monochrome value
comparisons, all88 real faces and full inspections, filters/deep links, notes/export,
phone portrait/landscape and original projection/state equality. Run docs,
architecture, source reachability, assets and build checks; `npm start` for the
verified local preview. Detailed QA artifacts stay in ignored `artifacts/`.
