# Timegraph scroll

Generated on 2026-09-08 using the built-in image generation tool. The original
PNG, including its alpha channel, is saved as `images/dark-fantasy/timegraph-scroll.png`.
The frame and vellum are a single illustrated asset; Pixi renders the live controls,
legend, axes, and timeline on top. No source game textures are shipped.

Exact final prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production-ready 2D game UI parchment scroll panel texture, original illustrated asset. Create a wide 3:1 horizontal unfurled parchment scroll, viewed perfectly straight-on, orthographic, for a civilization timegraph. Inspired by the tactile hand-painted interfaces of Age of Empires II, Baldur's Gate II and Diablo II: weathered antique vellum, restrained dark bronze engraved vine-knot corner fittings, slim rolled parchment top and bottom edges, small wooden scroll endcaps at far left and right. The entire central 88 percent width and 76 percent height is clean pale warm ivory parchment with subtle fine fibres and almost invisible mottling, highly readable background for dark graph lines. Edges lightly browned with realistic paper curls and thin cast shadows. Fine detailed illustrated craftsmanship, worn low-saturation brass, no large ornaments, no skulls, no figures, no scenery. Panel should fill entire canvas edge to edge, no surrounding background or margins; corner hardware must stay within canvas. Flat rectangular silhouette, frame only occupies very narrow outer rim. No text, no letters, no labels, no symbols, no graph, no grid, no buttons. 1536x512 landscape.
