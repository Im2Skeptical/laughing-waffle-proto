## Findings that change the binding

1. `runner.inspectRetainedStates()` cannot inspect Life's private `draft`, `job.stateData`, `job.payload.stateData` or `nodes[*].stateData`. Add a separate passive, expensive Life inspector; do not route it through existing Life getters.
2. The full staged **GameState** and the displayed staged **tableau** are different objects. Purchases record reservations in the draft. `getVassalNodeDecisionPresentation` derives the board and completed-board readings; its temporary `tableauState` is not a retained forecast GameState. Check both the full draft and the actual retained rendered decision.
3. The current J6 confirm endpoint is a recap after paid-time commitment. It is not evidence of a browsable new arbitrary future. For these fixtures the reliable follow-up targets are committed history16/17 after recap dismissal, plus Present54/54/64. Historical release can install the viewed state in the runner **cursor**, with no drag preview.
4. Existing frozen exports suffice for independent **logical** action-prefix replay, paid-time history and subsequent official ticks. They do not export the full decision-presentation derivation or direct `applyAction` transaction operation. A new, separately pinned SDK extension can re-export those operations from already captured baseline files. Do not regenerate or overwrite the immutable15 fixtures.
5. All three immutable recorded purchases currently have index0, but select by the recorded action's offer ID and validate its index in the recorded shop inventory. The generator's contract is first `canStage`, not first inventory entry.
6. Forecast stale-generation control does not exercise Life's direct commit guard. A controlled response can retain an actual old ready Life job/base pair and re-present it to the unchanged `commitResolution` guard after a genuine commit has made it stale; require rejection, zero runner commit calls, and full unchanged state/RNG. Separately replay old Life worker envelopes at the actual message gate.
